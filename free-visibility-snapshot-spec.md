# Free Visibility Snapshot Spec

## Stage 1 Goal

Create a low-friction front door for Verity Works that lets a business owner request a short visibility preview before committing to Verity Scan + Focused Fix.

## Offer Ladder

1. Free Visibility Snapshot
2. Verity Scan + Focused Fix starting at $1,500
3. Additional scoped implementation or ongoing readiness support

## Page Location

Public URL:

```text
https://verityworks.dev/free-visibility-snapshot/
```

Local file:

```text
public/free-visibility-snapshot/index.html
```

## Snapshot Promise

The Snapshot is a short outside-in preview of:

- what the website clearly communicates
- what may be confusing
- the likely first fix
- whether Verity Scan + Focused Fix is worth doing

It is not a ranking report, traffic forecast, lead guarantee, or full-site audit.

## Stage 1 Intake

The first version uses a prepared email request instead of a backend form. Fields:

- business website
- business name
- requester email
- optional context about what changed or feels worth checking

Reason: keep launch simple while testing which requests and findings convert.

## Fulfillment Notes

Manual Snapshot output should stay short:

- business reviewed
- one-paragraph summary
- what looks clear
- what looks unclear
- first priority
- recommended next step

Target delivery can be email, a small HTML Snapshot page, or a short call-prep note depending on the prospect.
