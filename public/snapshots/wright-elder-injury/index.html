Bottom line

The firm is clearer than the website structure.

Wright reads like a highly experienced elder injury and serious injury law firm. The site still makes visitors and machines work to connect the firm name, attorney story, practice areas, service area, and best contact path into one clear model.

The opportunity is to tighten the page structure so a visitor can quickly answer: who handles these cases, what kinds of injuries are covered, where the firm serves, how do I start, and what should search and AI systems associate with the firm?

What is already working

This is not a rescue project.

The snapshot reviewed the homepage, Our Story, Law Practice Areas, Contact, and attorney bio paths. It found reachable pages, a clear legal theme, visible attorney credibility, and concrete injury scenarios.

Strength

Strong attorney story

Our Story names H. Micheal Wright and Lincoln M. Wright and gives the firm a credible experience anchor.

Strength

Concrete case types

Law Practice Areas names nursing home negligence, assisted living negligence, medical malpractice, wrongful death, and related injury scenarios.

Strength

A first step exists

The site points visitors toward a free consultation, phone contact, and a form-based contact path.

Where it gets fuzzy

The site asks visitors to assemble the story themselves.

The important ingredients are present. They need a clearer hierarchy so families and machines can both understand the firm without reconciling scattered signals.

Signal gap

Structured data is missing

No JSON-LD was detected on the reviewed pages. A clean LegalService entity connected to attorneys, services, and contact actions would make the firm easier to interpret.

Local gap

Service area needs a clearer anchor

Arizona credentials and case history are visible, but city, address, and service-area signals should be easier to verify.

Content gap

Practice areas need more depth

The services list is useful. The highest-value topics need more answer-ready context around warning signs, evidence, timelines, and next steps.

Trust gap

Trust proof should be easier to verify

The site describes experience and specialization. Credential, profile, review, and case-history signals should be easier to corroborate from the page structure.

Proposed sprint

Wright Clarity Sprint

A compact sprint would focus on the highest-leverage cleanup: local identity, practice-area hierarchy, conversion path clarity, and structured data alignment. The point is to make the current site easier to find, understand, and act on, not to rebuild everything.

1

Confirm the primary firm facts

Lock the preferred business name, phone, address, city, attorneys, service area, and primary practice categories.

2

Clarify the practice-area hierarchy

Separate core elder injury work from adjacent serious injury categories so the strongest topics do not flatten into a long list.

3

Tighten contact and intake paths

Make the free consultation, phone number, contact form, and next step consistent across the pages where families are likely to decide.

4

Align schema with the real services

Add structured data for the firm, website, attorneys, services, and contact actions, using only claims visitors can verify on the page.

5

Hand over a before/after note

Close the sprint with a short summary of what changed, why it matters, and what the firm can build on next.

Growth channels

Traffic work comes after the foundation is clear.

Traffic growth is a separate layer. Backlinks, PR, local content, paid search, and AI-platform advertising experiments may be worth evaluating, but only after the firm’s core signals are clear. This cleanup is designed to make the site easier for people and machines to interpret; it does not guarantee rankings, leads, backlinks, press coverage, or placement in AI answers.

The practical sequence is foundation first, amplification second: clarify the firm and services, make the site machine-readable, improve the client action path, then decide whether traffic, PR, backlinks, or paid channels are worth pursuing.
